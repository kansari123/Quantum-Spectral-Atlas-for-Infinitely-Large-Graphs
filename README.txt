Ancillary materials for: Quantum Spectral Atlases for Exponentially Large Graphs (K. Ansari, 2026)

Contents:
- expG/, expH/, expG2/ : explicit-graph and implicit-scale experiments (code, per-run results, archived datasets)
- expI/ : signed and magnetic operator experiments (code, results, path-sampler baselines)
- expJ/ : quantum-comparison worked constants and cost tables
- qpvl_ibm_hardware_validation.ipynb : IBM hardware acquisition + analysis notebook (Sec. Hardware characterization)
- qpvl_run2_reanalysis_cell.py : self-contained zero-QPU reanalysis of the cached hardware run

All accuracy criteria were fixed prior to data collection; seeds are listed in the paper's Data availability section. Ground truth is dual-generator with cross-checks at or below 1e-9.
